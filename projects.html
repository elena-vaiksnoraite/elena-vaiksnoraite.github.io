Measuring the prosody of speaker commitments

Python R Praat Data cleaning Data visualization

When a person states that "Perhaps John stopped smoking", it is often assumed that the speaker is commited to the fact that "John used to smoke". This is not necessarily the case. This paper asks whether people "sound" different when they commit to a fact versus simply speculate about it. Fourteen participants read short stories alous. I processed 114 recordings by extracting acoustic properties of the signal in Praat, hand-correcting the automated output, and annotating intonational patterns with blinded coders. I then fit linear and logistic mixed-effect models in R (lme4) with random effects for speakerand sentence to account for individual variation, normalizing for speech rate and pitch range across participants. The analysis surfaced three statistically significant signals. This project shows my approach to data work: careful experimental design, rigorous cleaning and exclusion criteria, appropriate statistical modelling and translating subtle patterns in messy real-world data into clear, defensible conlusions. Measuring language variation with survey data: What 94 speakers of Russian reveal about case usage

Data cleaning Data visualization R PCIbex

In Russian there are two ways to say "cat" in the sentence "I didn't see a cat": cat can be accusative or genitive (literally "of cat"). These two cases are in competition. I designed and ran an online experiment to test whether definiteness and existential commitment drive a long-debated grammatical choice in Russian. I designed and ran an online acceptability-rating experiment testing what drives the case alternation. Recrtuitment through word of mouth and social media yielded a large but heterogenous pool. so I applied strict inclusion criteria to get a cleaner data set. My two hypotheses about definiteness and existential commitment were not supported, but the null result was informative. Exploratory analysis revealed larger effects. Specifically, the experiment provided evidence of an ongoing language change.
