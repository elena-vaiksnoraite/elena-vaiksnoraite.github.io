Measuring speaker commitments

Python R Praat Data normalization Data visualization

Do speakers "sound" different when they're committed to something versus merely speculating about it? In this project, we asked 14 university students to read short stories aloud, each containing a sentence like "Perhaps she was aware that he was unreliable". In one version of the story, the speaker clearly believes the embedded fact (he is unreliable). In the other, the speaker only speculates. Careful acoustic analysis of 114 recordings showed that the speakers subtly changed how they said the target sentence. Learn more.→

Measuring language variation in Russian

Data cleaning Data visualization R PCIbex

I designed and deployed an online survey to test whether two contextual factors drive a long-debated choice between two grammatical forms in Russian. Recruitment through social media produced a large but heterogenous sample, so I applied strict inclusion and data-quality criteria. The hypotheses weren't supported but segmenting responses by item and participant revealed larger effects, including a strong age-based pattern that points to an ongoing shift in how language is used.
